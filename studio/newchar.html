2. Who they are

The source image is a starting point, not the identity. Measured here: an image used as a live identity reference transports hair mass, garment colour and the presence of eyewear — a costume, not a person — and the face is never quite theirs. So the picture is used the two ways that do work: it is captioned into words, and it is redrawn into your idiom. Words survive every engine; an image reference does not.

The redraw matters more than it looks. A reference sheet imports its style and its medium into everything downstream, so a sheet that is still a photograph quietly turns the whole film photographic.

What you get is a draft: a description, a sheet, and rough tags to edit. Nothing is measured yet — no turnaround, no LoRA, no verdicts. If the sheet looks right, the turnaround is the next step.
