# The Cartoon-Intro Set — five versions of the waltz you liked

All five keep the DNA of `theme_waltz` — **6/8 time, warm female lead, singable** — and push
it toward a title-sequence theme: a hook you can chant, the name of the place sung out
loud, and an ending you can cut a title card on.

All five landed on **seed 48**, the same seed as the waltz you picked, and all five held
level across the full 62 seconds on the first try (no fade — see the tail check in
`music/uplifting-set.md`). Every file is normalised to −16 LUFS.

Files in `music/cartoon-intro/`. Reproducible from `build/make_cartoon_theme.py`.

---

## The lyric they all sing

A cartoon theme chants the name. Short lines, one image each, hook every four bars.

> **Verse**
> Down on Store Street, second door,
> green as green and gold on the sign —
> there's a pickle on the wall who's been dancing since before
> you or me or anyone in line.
>
> **CHORUS**
> **Sour Pickle! Sour Pickle Cafe!**
> Ain't a sour thing about it, no way.
> Big plate, warm plate, come as you are —
> the little cafe that'll make your day.
>
> **Verse**
> She grew the lettuce in her own back yard,
> he's been cracking the eggs since dawn.
> Nothing here is fast and nothing here is hard,
> just good and hot and gone.
>
> **CHORUS** *(repeat)*
>
> **Outro**
> The little cafe that'll make your day.

## The five

**A · `A_waltz_plus.mp3` — 68 bpm, D major**
Your waltz, just bigger. Same fingerpicked nylon guitar, plus accordion, upright bass and
brushed drums arriving on the chorus, with a few friends joining in. The safest step up
from what you already liked — still intimate, now singable.

**B · `B_saturday_morning.mp3` — 76 bpm, D major** ← *used on the intro cut*
The full Saturday-morning cartoon: brass stabs, oompah tuba, playful clarinet, xylophone
runs, big ensemble chorus, ending on a held chord you can drop a title card onto. The most
literal read of "cartoon intro."

**C · `C_music_hall.mp3` — 72 bpm, G major**
Vaudeville. Honky-tonk upright piano, tuba, banjo, wire-brush snare. Comic and bouncy,
like a pub piano at closing time. The funniest of the five and the best match for the
pickle's personality.

**D · `D_big_singalong.mp3` — 70 bpm, F major**
Nylon guitar and piano with strings swelling on the chorus, glockenspiel, and a children's
choir joining in. Anthemic — the end credits of a beloved cartoon rather than the opening.

**E · `E_pure_waltz_long.mp3` — 68 bpm, D major**
The *exact* recipe of the take you liked, just at full length with the new chant lyric.
If your instinct was "don't touch it, just give me more of it," this is that.

## Which to use

**C** for character and **B** for the title sequence. C is the one that sounds like this
particular cafe — slightly ramshackle, funny, warm — where B is the one that sounds like a
cartoon. **E** is the control: if C and B both feel like too much, E proves the sparse
version still works with the new hook.

The intro cut ships with **B** under it (`deliverables/sourpickle_intro_20s_scored.mp4`),
and unscored (`sourpickle_intro_20s.mp4`) so you can drop any of the five under it yourself.

**Note on the cut:** the intro is 20s and the songs are 62s, so the scored version plays
the *verse*, not the chorus. For a real title sequence you'd cut the picture to land on
"Sour Pickle! Sour Pickle Cafe!" — which is a music-edit decision worth making once the
shot list is final.
