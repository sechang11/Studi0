# "The Little Cafe That'll Make Your Day" — Sour Pickle Cafe theme song

Built from the cafe's own tagline. Written to work three ways: a full ~1:50 song for social,
a 30-second cut for the commercial, and a 5-second sung logo (the "sonic mnemonic") that can end
every piece of Sour Pickle media forever.

---

## Musical direction

| | |
|---|---|
| Feel | Front-porch shuffle. Think a Canadian diner cousin of "You Are My Sunshine" — swung eighths, brushes on a snare, upright bass, honky-tonk piano, a lazy pedal-steel or slide guitar answering the vocal. |
| Tempo | 104 BPM, 4/4 with a triplet swing |
| Key | G major (bright, singable by anyone in a car) |
| Progression | Verse: `G – G – C – G` / `G – D – G – (D7)` · Chorus: `C – G – A7 – D7` / `G – C – G – D7 – G` |
| Voice | One warm alto lead, slightly untrained, smiling. A small ragged "family" chorus (3–5 voices, some of them kids) on the hook only. |
| Length | Full 1:50 · Commercial cut 0:30 · Sung logo 0:05 |
| Production note | Record it to sound like a 1970s local-TV jingle that was *loved*, not a polished modern spot. Room tone, a little tape hiss, no click-track perfection. |

---

## Full lyric

**Verse 1**
> Eight in the morning on Store Street,
> Bridge is still shaking off the rain,
> There's a green fella grinning on a swinging sign
> Been grinning there since who-knows-when.

**Verse 2**
> She knows how you take your bacon,
> He's already cracking your eggs,
> No app, no line, no menu you can't read —
> Just come in and sit down and stay.

**CHORUS**
> Oh, the *Sour* Pickle —
> ain't a sour thing about it!
> Just a little green door
> and a plate you can't finish, and more.
> Twenty-some years of the same good morning,
> from a garden out back to your fork —
> **the little cafe that'll make your day.**

**Verse 3**
> Lettuce she grew in the summer,
> Tomatoes still warm from the vine,
> Peppers and squash and whatever came up —
> that's what's going on the plate tonight. *(→ "…on your plate this time.")*

**Bridge** *(half-time, just piano and voice)*
> Everything's made when you order it,
> so honey, that coffee's on us —
> Read a book about pickles,
> watch the bridge go up,
> nothin' worth having comes fast enough.

**CHORUS (full, family voices in)**

**Outro / tag**
> Sixteen-twenty-three Store —
> we'll leave the light on and the pot on the stove.
> *(spoken, smiling)* …and yes, we'll cut the pickle in half so you can share it.

## 0:05 sung logo

Three notes and a wink — the only part that must never change:

> **"Sour Pick-le Ca-fé…"** *(G – B – D – E, landing on)* **"…makes your day."** *(D – G)*

Whistled version for cold opens; kazoo version for the pickle mascot's own scenes.

---

## Alternate angle — "Grandma's Kitchen But It's Downtown"

If the shuffle feels too country for Victoria, the other lane that fits the reviews is a slow,
fingerpicked 6/8 waltz in D, one voice and one nylon-string guitar, no drums. Same chorus lyric,
half the tempo. It turns the spot from "charming local ad" into "short film about a place that's
still here." Recommend cutting a demo of both and choosing on picture.
