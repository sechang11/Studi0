Fill the library

Generate random content on its own for a set number of hours. Nothing to write — every job is drawn from the measured card libraries, so it inherits what they know about what works.

How long

A budget is required. This cannot be started without one.

Why the deadline is required. An earlier generation loop on this box had no stopping condition and ran for 17 hours 22 minutes, holding the GPU and starving three other jobs, with nothing on screen to show it was running. The budget is enforced by the script itself, not just by this page — and it will never begin a job it cannot finish, so a video roll in the last two minutes becomes an image instead of being killed halfway.

The mix

Each weight is how many tickets that type gets in the bag. Every job draws one, so these are proportions rather than counts.

Video costs roughly fifteen times an image: it renders a keyframe first, looks at it, and only then moves it. That is deliberate — a rejected keyframe costs six seconds instead of ninety.

What it draws from

Counted from the cards on disk, not from a number typed into this page.

reading the libraries…

Only cards that were rendered and looked at are drawable. The known duds are excluded by name with their reason: the night grade clips to black rather than darkening, three camera moves are byte-identical to static, and a style card measured to put a prop in the subject's hands never gets drawn.

Run

You can close this page — the run is a separate process and keeps going.

not running 
idle.

Near-black renders are rejected into a rejected/ folder with the reason recorded rather than deleted — a failure you can look at is worth more than one you cannot. Every artifact keeps a sidecar with the exact cards and seed behind it, so anything good can be made again.
