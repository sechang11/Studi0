The two sections

    
## MUST NEVER CHANGE

     The promises. These do not move without a conversation.

    
## CAN CHANGE

     Flair — timings, which take, intensities. Free to move.

    
Write MAKE PERMANENT under anything in
     CAN CHANGE and it moves up when you save.

   

   

    
Locking a shot

    
Lock records which take was picked at that moment.
     After that the editor won't save over it, and run checks fails loudly if the
     film ever points at a different take.

    
That's the part that matters — a finished shot
     is usually lost by something quietly re-picking underneath it, not by someone
     editing its words.
