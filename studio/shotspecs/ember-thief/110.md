# Shot 110 - the uppercut

## WHAT HAPPENS

The turn: his trick, her counter - and the pendant is free. It flies up into 120. (shot 11 of 20, after 100, before 120)

## BUILT WITH

frames first in the clearing set (set_film.py frames), H3 between the set's two frames (set_film.py takes) - the camera, the marks and the facing come from the set

## MUST NEVER CHANGE

*These are the promises. They do not change without a conversation.*

### Terra on the left, the jester on the right
Terra is on the LEFT of the screen and the jester on the RIGHT, for the whole shot.
WHY: one line of action for the whole film: every camera stands east of the line between them, so a swap of sides would read as a jump
CHECK: by eye

### They face each other
Terra faces screen-right (toward him); the jester faces screen-left (toward her), as the set's frames draw them.
WHY: the duel's frames had them mirrored (set_test.py read the sheets' turnaround names the wrong way round until 2026-10-05) - a take must keep the facing its frames give
CHECK: by eye

### It starts where the last shot ended
Terra starts where 100 left her.
WHY: each shot is caused by the one before it - the director's note on the duel was shots strung together like separate scenes
CHECK: by eye

### He appears in FRONT of her
Out of a puff of purple smoke, on her right (screen right) - never behind her.
WHY: the script (studio/shotscripts/ember-thief.md)
CHECK: by eye

### The chain snaps
Her uppercut breaks the chain and the pendant flies UP.
WHY: the script (studio/shotscripts/ember-thief.md)
CHECK: by eye

### Length
4.38 seconds (105 frames at 24 fps).
WHY: the cut is timed to the story beats
CHECK: duration between 3.98 and 4.98
