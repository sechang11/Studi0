# Shot 030 — He lands in his own outline and the shop lights up

## WHAT HAPPENS

The hanging sign is EMPTY - just an outline where the pickle should be. He tumbles in from above, lands exactly in it, and the shop lights up behind.

## BUILT WITH

build/sign_light.py · assets/2-1.jpg, graded in-script

## MUST NEVER CHANGE

*These are the promises. I do not change one without telling you first.*

### Sign starts empty
The shot opens on the sign with the pickle ABSENT and only his outline drawn on the board.
WHY: It is the whole gag: the sign is waiting for him.

### Landing is pixel exact
He lands on exactly the rectangle he was removed from, so the merge is a cross-fade onto the untouched photograph.
WHY: Both the hole and the landing are generated from one measured rect, so they cannot drift apart. An approximate landing reads as a cut.
CHECK: provenance mentions outline

### Mirrored to this side
The flying figure is mirrored and colour-drifted to the sign's turquoise side.
WHY: 1-2.jpg and 2-1.jpg show OPPOSITE sides of the sign - different facing AND different paint. Unmirrored, he arrives backwards and the wrong colour.

### Lights after landing
The lights come on INSIDE the store - masked to the glazing - and only AFTER he lands.
WHY: Grading the whole shopfront reads as an exposure change. Light behind the glass reads as somebody flicking a switch, which is the point.
