# Shot 050 — Inside, the room turns past us and she is behind the counter

## WHAT HAPPENS

We are inside the cafe. The camera turns steadily through the room — the yellow wall and
the window tables, the brick wall with its chalkboards and pictures — and settles on the
counter, where the owner is standing.

## MUST NEVER CHANGE

*These are the promises. I do not change one without telling you first.*

### The inside is photographs
Every frame is one of the cafe's own interior photographs, panned across. Nothing in this
shot is generated, extended or filled in.
WHY: The four photographs were taken from four different places, so they cannot be
stitched into a real panorama — and inventing the joins would mean inventing the inside of
the cafe, which this film has never done.

### It reads as one continuous turn
The motion never stops and never reverses. The joins are cross-dissolves timed to land
mid-move, so the eye reads rotation rather than four cuts.

### It ends on her at the counter
The last view is 5-1.jpg and it eases to a stop with the owner behind the counter. She is
what the shot is for.
