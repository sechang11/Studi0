Start here

This studio makes short films on this computer, with no account and no bill. You choose people, places and what happens; it draws and animates them. You never have to write a prompt. Three steps.

1

Choose your people

Every card on the Characters page is a person you can put in a film. Their face, body, turnaround and expressions are already made, so they look the same in every shot.

  • Want someone new? Click the + card. Describe them in a sentence, or pick traits from lists (age, build, hair, clothes). Building a new person takes about two minutes.
  • You can also start from a photo, but only of someone who has agreed to it. The studio asks you to confirm that.
  • A level 1 badge means the person is ready to cast.
Open Characters
2

Choose your places

A place is a set: a street at night, a harbour in fog, a shrine in a forest. Each comes with several background pictures (wide, reverse, detail) so a scene can cut between angles and stay in the same place.

  • The Foundry page lists places, props and costumes. Making a new place from lists takes about a minute; it renders its own pictures.
  • Props (a lantern, a book, a radio) can sit in a shot. A prop a person must hold should be part of the person instead: say so when you describe them.
Open the Foundry
3

Make a film

In the film editor: new film, give it a name, choose the look (photoreal or anime) and the resolution. Then, for each scene:

  • Add the scene, pick where it happens (a place and a background picture), tick who is present, save.
  • Click generate coverage. The studio lays out the usual shots for you: the wide, the two-shot, a close on each person, an insert. The people are already in the pictures.
  • On each shot, write what happens in one sentence, pick a motion if someone moves (crouch, turn, look up), add a spoken line if someone speaks, then press Make this shot. It tells you what it is doing in plain words.
  • When every shot is made, assemble on the Review tab. Music is chosen per scene from a few words.
Open the film editor
What to expect. A shot takes one to three minutes to make. A film of eight shots is about twenty minutes of waiting, which you can spend writing the next scene. The Review tab tells you, per shot, whether it can be made here as asked, whether it will differ from what you asked (and why), or whether it needs an outside service. If a shot comes out wrong, press Make this shot again: each attempt is kept and you pick the one you like.
