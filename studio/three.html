The bobblehead line

A bobblehead is two printed solids and a spring. This section builds the bodies — a seed image in figurine grammar, meshed through Hunyuan3D at the recipe measured on this box, cut at the neck, bored for the spring — and the heads, supersized off a photograph and seated on a matching socket.

Every card below states what was measured, not what was intended: face count, whether the solid is watertight, the joint diameter the cut actually found, and whether mesh_doctor will call it printable.

How the two parts meet

Both sockets are blind holes with a 1.2 mm chamfered mouth, bored after the part is scaled to millimetres — so 8.4 mm is 8.4 mm on the thing that gets sliced.
 


  

    
Assembled 

    
A body, a spring and a head stacked at their real heights — the one
       view the two libraries cannot give you on their own. Preview only: the three
       parts print separately, which is the whole point of the socket, and the spring here
       is drawn rather than modelled. Build one with
       python3 studio/_tools/assemble.py --body <id> --head <id>.

    

  


  
Bodies 

  
Seeded from a shared figurine grammar — hair as one closed mass, arms
     close to the body, no held thin objects, no cast shadow — then meshed, cut and
     socketed. The head you see in the seed image is removed; what ships is the body
     below the neck.

  

  

  

  


  
Figures 

  
One photograph of a whole person, meshed once and cut at the neck
     into a matched head and body. Meshing once is the point: two separate generations of
     the same person agree on nothing, so the joint between them is a guess — one mesh
     cut in half has one neck, and both sockets are bored to it. If the photo is cut off,
     the figurine pass invents what is missing and the card says so.

Heads 

  
A photograph, matted and squared, meshed at the face voxel
     resolution, flattened under the jaw and bored for the same spring. This is the part
     that carries the likeness, so it is the part that gets the fine voxel.


  
  

    

      

        
        
Drop a photograph here

          or click to choose one — jpg or png, face-on, whole head in frame

      

      
      
The pipeline runs on one front-facing photo. It does not need a
         turnaround, a clean background or studio light — the figurine pass strips the
         background, flattens the lighting and closes the hair before anything is meshed,
         because Hunyuan3D sculpts what it sees and a hard shadow becomes a groove.

    

    

      

        

      

        
        mm — supersize it here

      

        

      
Delights the photo, closes the hair into one sculpted
         mass and crops to the head. Both source images are kept so you can compare.

      
      
About three minutes: figurine pass, matte, Hunyuan3D, repair, smooth,
         flatten under the jaw, bore the socket, export.
