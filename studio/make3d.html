Not a thing this model does. The only multi-image path into Hunyuan3D is the MultiView node, and it is inert without the 2mv checkpoint. Blending two images into one "inspired" shape is not a supported mode — CLIP-vision conditioning describes one object, and a contact sheet of several was measured to come back as 1.16M faces of floating slabs. WHAT DOES WORK Merge at the image stage, where merging is a solved problem: make one picture of the thing you want on the image engines, then mesh that. The mesh model only ever sees one picture, so give it the picture you actually want.
