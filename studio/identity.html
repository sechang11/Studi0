What you are doing here

  1. Each card shows a character's reference sheet (left, “them”) against what one method produced (right, “this”).
  2. Click the images to open them large side by side. Esc, the ×, or a click outside closes it. You can judge from inside that view too.
  3. Pick a verdict. Clicking a different button changes your answer; clicking the same one again clears it. Nothing is final.

The question is not whether they match pixel for pixel. It is: would you accept these as the same character in the same film?

keep You would use this. It reads as them.
close Recognisably them, one specific thing off — hairstyle, age, costume. The identity landed and a detail did not. Counts as a pass for the method and points at the character's tags instead.
not them A different person. Different bone structure or colouring; nothing you could cut together with the reference.

Different style — photoreal reference, anime render? Not an identity failure. Judge what survives a change of medium: hair colour, eye colour, build, age, scars, glasses, marks.

Genuinely can’t tell? Leave it blank. Unjudged is counted honestly as “not looked at” and costs nothing; a guess puts a number under a verdict nobody made. Several unjudgeable for the same reason is itself worth telling me — it usually means the reference is bad.

Three seeds per character per method. The same ask rendered three times, so one lucky picture cannot pass for a reliable method.
