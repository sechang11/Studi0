# Review: THE FIRE ESPER (2026-10-05)

Graded from the glued cut (a frame a second, and a strip across every glued join), the takes behind it (eight
frames across each, two to four takes a shot) and the set's frames. The brief, the director's note after THE EMBER
THIEF: "the fighting sequence still has many moves that don't connect with one another... a multi-shot sequence
where terra is using a hand praying incantation and magic energy circles her... a demonic esper spirit that comes
out to fight joker... each shot prior has to tell a story leading up to the next shot" - with transitions that
glue the shots together, and a name for the method. Grades on [the book's scale](../README.md#grades).

## What keeps it from an A

- The wides: 010, 140 and 230 are the right shots for where we are, but the figures are 130-210 px tall.
- Two keys came out soft: 050's circle is paler than 040's (and the editor moved the camera in), 220's glow does
  not fade far.
- 070's birds are dots.

## Second pass (the same afternoon)

The director on the cut: the characters carried "some distortion of pixels... I think it's a bug" (Terra's sash and
the jester's ruff in 010; both whole figures in 140), and the score candidates did not "sound like music as I'm
used to". Both were real:
- **The mosaic** was H3's turbo LoRA at 4 steps drawing a triangle lattice over small figures from the take's first
  frame (`pipe-first-last`, LTX_PLAYBOOK §101.6 point 8). Every take re-rendered at 12 steps, both seeds, and
  re-picked: 060 (a ring of glowing runes now circles her as she prays) and 220 (the glow flares in a ring of runes,
  then dims) improved to A and A-; 110 and 150 changed seeds; the rest kept their seed. The wides are clean.
- **The music**: the note was about the TerraTheme covers (round 4, on the larger model), not this film's
  score. A music-forward remix made for it was "noticeably worse" and is undone: the score candidates are mixed as
  before, the takes' own sound leading (`trans-sound-bridge`).
Still keeping it from an A: the small wides (010, 140, 230), 050's paler circle, 070's dot-sized birds.
