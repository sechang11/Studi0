# Review: the five challenge films of 2026-09-30

Graded after the fact, from contact sheets of each final (a frame every 1.6 s for the short films and
every 3.5 s for the long ones) and from the takes behind them. Each brief is broken into the things it
asked for. Each ask gets the shot that answered it, the engine and seed of the take in the cut, and a
grade on [the book's scale](../README.md#grades). A failure names the entry where its lesson now
lives.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| SYSTEM ERROR | 44.7 s + title | 10 | **B+** | the three named challenges pass; the detective's glowing eyes and the pull-in do not |
| THE SMALLEST GEAR | 45.2 s + title | 10 | **B** | four hands and knit work; the look is CG, not stop-motion; the dust motes do not read |

## What the five grades say together

1. **Camera moves asked for in words are faked.** The orbit, the 360 circle in flight, tracking the
   fingers, the dolly zoom, the pull-in: every engine arcs a little, pushes or cross-fades. Every
   camera move that worked was **built**, either by Blender previz under LTX-2.3's IC-LoRA or by first-last
   frame between two composed frames. The Courier's two D grades are both moves that were asked for
   and not built.
2. **Physical interaction goes to H3.** Steam fogging glasses, spray off keys, a hand guiding a hand,
   the POV vault, the shatter, the splash. LTX turned three of these into something else: the piano
   exploded, the vault became a stranger's somersault, gears rose out of the watch.
3. **Identity comes from Qwen-Image-2.1 compositing the references; framing comes from Flux 2.**
   Macros, ECUs and inserts drawn by Qwen came back too wide; faces drawn by Flux 2 drifted.
4. **Asymmetric features flip or double** (the circuit arm, a second silver eye, the silver iris on the
   wrong side for a frame). Fix it in the reference before anything is drawn, then check every close-up.
5. **Lighting has to make sense or the figure looks pasted.** Front-lit against a setting sun read as
   a cut-out until she was redrawn backlit.
6. **Small things vanish at delivery size**: dust motes, a sleeve's glow in silhouette, a HUD glitch.
7. **A style asked for in words drifts to the engines' default look.** Stop-motion came out as CG.
