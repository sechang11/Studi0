| family | status | last tested | best result |
|---|---|---|---|
| framing | proven | 2026-09-30 | quantum-courier 104, 306; cyber-alchemist 303, grade A- |

**Also called:** low-angle shot, looking up at, hero angle, 45-degree low angle, low angle wide, low angle push
**Not the same as:**
- [`frame-low-angle-ground`](low-angle-ground.md) - the camera ON the ground, framing feet
- [`frame-high-angle`](high-angle.md) - looking down

## Recipe (v1, 2026-09-30)

Write the angle as a number and a direction in the start frame ("a LOW ANGLE TRACKING SHOT looking up at forty-five degrees at the woman"); LTX, or H3 for a figure rushing at the camera.

## Checks before picking

- The angle holds for the whole take (it does not flatten to eye level).

## Progression

### 2026-09-05 · §55 · angles performed on the plate · grade A-
- **Did:** the builder's keystone plus a camera drop inside a zoom; the angle in prose for comparison.
- **Got:** exact to 0.02 wherever the ruler can read; "an angle put into the PLATE is copied" (6 of 6 takes read the angle asked). In prose the engine returned eye level. The figure seen from below came out a big head on a short body.
- **Learned:** put the angle into the start frame; the figure's angle stays unsolved there.
