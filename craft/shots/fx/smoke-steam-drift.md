# Smoke and steam drifting (`fx-smoke-steam-drift`)

| family | status | last tested | best result |
|---|---|---|---|
| fx | proven | 2026-09-30 | cyber-alchemist 101, 111; system-error 030, grade A- |

**Also called:** drifting smoke, volumetric smoke, steam rising, haze, atmosphere
**Not the same as:**
- [`fx-steam-on-glass`](steam-on-glass.md) - steam condensing on a surface; drifting smoke changes nothing it touches
- [`fx-dust-motes`](dust-motes.md) - separate specks in a beam; smoke is a continuous haze

## Recipe (v1, 2026-09-30)

Put the smoke in the start frame and name its direction; both engines carry it.

1. The place reference and the start frame already hold the smoke or steam.
2. Either engine.
3. Prompt: where it comes from and where it drifts ("smoke drifts across the lens").

## Checks before picking

- The smoke moves slowly and one way; it does not pulse.
