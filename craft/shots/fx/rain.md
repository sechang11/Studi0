| family | status | last tested | best result |
|---|---|---|---|
| fx | proven | 2026-09-30 | system-error 020, 080; storm-sonata 030, grade A- |

**Also called:** rain, downpour, thunderstorm rain, rain on glass, rain streaks, raindrops
**Not the same as:**
- [`fx-surface-spray`](surface-spray.md) - droplets thrown UP off a surface; rain falls
- [`fx-reflective-surface`](reflective-surface.md) - the wet ground's reflections; rain is the falling water

## Recipe (v1, 2026-09-30)

Put the rain in the start frame and the place reference; either engine carries it. Rain ON glass and ON lacquer went to H3.

1. The place reference is drawn wet and raining.
2. For drops streaking on a window or bouncing on a surface, render on H3.
3. Prompt: where the rain lands and what it does there.

## Checks before picking

- The rain keeps falling for the whole take (does not stop mid-shot).
