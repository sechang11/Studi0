**Not the same as:**
- [`fx-zero-g-droplets`](zero-g-droplets.md) - liquid that splits and wobbles; here solid objects drift and tumble

## Recipe (v1, 2026-09-30)

A start frame with the objects already in the air around the figure, on LTX, with a slow crane.

1. Start frame: a wide with books, glassware and tools already floating (Qwen-Image-2.1 with the character and the place).
2. LTX; H3 was similar but darker.
3. Prompt: what floats, and where it goes ("drifts slowly upward ... toward the vaulted ceiling"), and what the figure does (turns slowly, watching).

## Checks before picking

- The objects keep drifting up for the whole take; none snaps back to the bench.

## Progression
