**Not the same as:**
- [`fx-reflective-surface`](reflective-surface.md) - what a glossy surface mirrors; lightning is the light event itself
- [`fx-electric-arc`](electric-arc.md) - energy discharging into a body, built in post

## Recipe (v1, 2026-09-30)

Lightning in the place reference's sky, and the flash timed in words to an action. H3 carried the strongest flash.

1. The place reference is a stormy sky with lightning.
2. Prompt: when the flash comes and what it lights (storm-sonata 070: "at the same instant lightning strikes behind the church and floods the square with white light").
3. H3 for a flash tied to an action.

## Checks before picking

- The flash lights the scene (the subject brightens), not only the sky.
