# How many named characters one frame can hold (`cont-cast-count`)

| family | status | last tested | best result |
|---|---|---|---|
| continuity | works-with-caveats | 2026-09-30 | three named at most; quantum-courier 107's crowd of unnamed extras, grade A- |

**Also called:** crowd of named characters, ensemble shot, group shot, many characters, party scene
**Not the same as:**
- [`frame-two-shot`](../framing/two-shot.md) - two people, the easy case
- [`cont-face-identity`](face-identity.md) - one face held over the film

## Recipe (v1, 2026-09-04)

At most three named characters who must stay themselves in one frame (two is safe); everyone else is an unnamed extra. A shot that needs more is split into shots, or is an API shot.

## Checks before picking

- Count the named characters, and check each one.
