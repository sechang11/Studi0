# Looking up (`move-look-up`)

| family | status | last tested | best result |
|---|---|---|---|
| motion | proven | 2026-09-30 | cyber-alchemist 206; builder `look_up` same person 0.70 → 0.70, grade A- |

**Also called:** looks up, glances, looks off, a small look, raises her eyes, looks at something above
**Not the same as:**
- [`move-turn-in-place`](turn-in-place.md) - the whole body or head turns and the face can leave the frame
- [`move-crouch`](crouch.md) - the body lowers
- [`move-gesture`](gesture.md) - a nod, a laugh, a raised hand

## Recipe (v1, 2026-09-04)

In words, on LTX: "looks up", "glances", "listens" keep the face. Never force a pin for a small pose change.

## Checks before picking

- The face stays in the frame and the same.

## Progression

### 2026-09-04 · §43 · small pose changes forced into pins · grade D
- **Did:** pins below the change floor (0.0012-0.0019/s) for look up, look off, nod, laugh.
- **Got:** the director judged every one "No": forced pins produce invention.
- **Learned:** below the floor, render on LTX with the motion in words.

### 2026-09-04 · §47, §49 · "looks up" in close-ups · grade A-
- **Did:** close-ups with "looks up, a small smile".
- **Got:** the face held through the take.
- **Learned:** small verbs keep the face.

- **Got:** a slow look up with the camera pushing in on the growing glow of her eye.
- **Learned:** as above.
