## Recipe (v1, 2026-09-30)

Draw both hands into the start frame, entering from the bottom edge, in her exact gloves; LTX.

1. Start frame: "two hands in [her gauntlets] ... gripping a steel railing at the edge of the colossal shaft", refs [place, her, her] (three references for Flux 2 compatibility). Qwen-Image-2.1 seed 11 was picked.
2. LTX; the grip in words.

## Checks before picking

- The hands enter from where her arms would be (bottom corners), both, with her gloves.
- The hands do not detach or float.
