**Not the same as:**

## Recipe (v1, 2026-09-30)

A start frame that is already the dive, with motion blur; LTX; the streaks in words.

1. Start frame: "a FIRST-PERSON FALLING SHOT straight down the narrow cyberpunk city canyon at terrifying speed, neon signs stretching into long streaks" ([place, place, place]).
2. LTX.
3. Prompt: the direction and speed, what streaks past.

## Checks before picking

- The motion keeps going the same way for the whole take (no reversal).
