## Recipe (v1, 2026-09-30)

H3 for a falling or diving figure; either engine for a runner's coat tails. Name the edges ("the edges staying sharp").

1. Start frame: the cloth already streaming behind or above her.
2. H3 for a fall (301, 302); LTX or H3 for running (202, 203).
3. Prompt: "The fabric whips and hammers in the wind: velvet coat tails, the patched cape and her long braid snapping violently, the edges staying sharp".

## Checks before picking

- No melting of cloth into skin (the brief's "clothes melting" failure); every garment keeps its edge.
- The cape does not cover the whole frame (LTX seed 202 of 302 spread it across the lens).
