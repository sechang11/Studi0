# Leading dolly (`cam-lead-dolly`)

| family | status | last tested | best result |
|---|---|---|---|
| camera | works-with-caveats | 2026-09-30 | quantum-courier 104, grade B+ |

**Also called:** leading shot, dolly backward, backing away in front, walk toward the camera tracked, reverse tracking
**Not the same as:**
- [`cam-track-alongside`](track-alongside.md) - beside her
- [`cam-pull-back`](pull-back.md) - backing away from a subject who stays put
- [`move-walk-toward-camera`](../motion/walk-toward-camera.md) - the walk itself; this is the camera's move in front of it

## Recipe (v1, 2026-09-30)

A start frame that is already the angle and the walk (a low-angle view of her mid-stride), the backward move in words, and a short take (the face redraws after about 4 s of walking, §55).

1. Start frame (104): "a LOW ANGLE TRACKING SHOT looking up at forty-five degrees at the woman of reference one walking straight toward the camera".
2. LTX; "She walks forward with purpose and the camera dollies backward in front of her at the same speed, low and tilted up".
3. 4 s.

## Checks before picking

- Her face at the end against her start frame (walks redraw faces).
