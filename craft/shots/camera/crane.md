# Crane (`cam-crane`)

| family | status | last tested | best result |
|---|---|---|---|
| camera | works-with-caveats | 2026-09-30 | cyber-alchemist 203, grade B+ |

**Also called:** crane, crane up, crane down, boom up, jib, pedestal, the camera rises, crane down to face
**Not the same as:**
- [`cam-crane-to-overhead`](crane-to-overhead.md) - a rise that ends looking straight down
- [`cam-tilt`](tilt.md) - the camera turns without rising

## Recipe (v2, 2026-09-30)

**/film builder:** a keystone post move ("a boom on a locked head": no parallax). **Shot-script pipeline:** LTX will rise with a subject that rises (droplets, a floating figure) when the words say so; for a rise past something, previz.

## Checks before picking

- The background changes with height (parallax) if the crane is meant to pass anything.

- **Did:** the angle keystone applied per frame with a ramping pitch, inside a zoom.

- **Learned:** a post crane is a tilt of the picture, not a rise of the camera.
